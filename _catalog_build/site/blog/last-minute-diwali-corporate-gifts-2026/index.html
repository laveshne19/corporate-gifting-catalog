Last-Minute Ordering Checklist

  1. Confirm stock availability — call or WhatsApp before finalising your list
  2. Share delivery locations — warehouse pickup vs. drop-shipping to employee addresses
  3. Decide on packaging — standard brand box (fast) vs. custom branded box (adds 5–7 days)
  4. Confirm GST details — provide GSTIN for B2B invoice
  5. Make advance payment — most distributors require 50–100% advance for large last-minute orders
