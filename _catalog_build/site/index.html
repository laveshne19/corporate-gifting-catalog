Corporate Gifting India — FAQ

Common questions from HR, procurement and admin teams ordering bulk corporate gifts in India.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for corporate gifts? +
MOQ varies by product category. Electronics like earbuds and smartwatches start at 50 units, kitchen appliances at 25–50 units, and premium items (large appliances, luggage) at 10–25 units. Contact us for exact MOQ and bulk pricing on your chosen products.
Do you deliver corporate gifts across India? +
Yes. We ship pan-India — Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Chandigarh, Ahmedabad, Kolkata and 100+ tier-2 cities. Custom packaging and branded gifting is available for all locations.
