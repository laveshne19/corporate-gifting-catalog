Handsets for Field and Operations Teams

Handsets for people on the move need to handle long days, navigation, CRM apps and photo uploads without constant charging. When choosing, compare battery capacity, RAM and storage, camera quality for documentation, display brightness outdoors, and the brand's software update support. Dual SIM support helps where employees keep separate personal and work numbers.

For operations teams such as delivery, service engineers or sales representatives, standardising on one or two models simplifies IT support and training. Budget-friendly smartphones for field staff from Realme, POCO, Lava and Motorola suit large deployments, while Samsung and Nothing handsets work well for managers and client-facing roles. Where simple calling is enough, a feature phone bulk order keeps costs down for supervisors, drivers or security staff.

Phones as Dealer Rewards and Employee Incentives

Smartphones are among the most motivating rewards for channel partners and sales teams.
