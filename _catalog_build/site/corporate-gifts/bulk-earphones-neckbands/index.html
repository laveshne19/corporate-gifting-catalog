Neckbands vs Wired Earphones

The choice between neckbands and wired earphones mostly comes down to budget and audience. Bluetooth neckbands suit sales teams, commuters and anyone who takes calls on the move. When comparing models, look at battery life, fast-charging support, driver size, microphone quality, water resistance rating and the magnetic earbud lock.

Wired earphones bulk orders make sense for very large distributions, such as retail schemes or event giveaways, where the per-unit budget is tight. They also suit recipients who prefer not to charge another device. Key specs to check include the connector type, since many newer phones lack a 3.5mm jack, cable durability and whether an in-line mic and controls are included. We can share a spec comparison of two or three shortlisted models before you decide.
