Comparing Models: Wattage, Jars and Build

Specifications matter more with appliances than with most gifts. Motor wattage affects how well the grinder handles tough masalas and idli or dosa batter, and heavier-duty motors suit families who grind daily. The number and type of jars matter too: a dry grinding jar, a wet grinding jar, a chutney jar and, in some models, a juicer attachment. Also look at speed settings, overload protection, jar material and cord length.

A juicer mixer grinder bulk order makes sense when recipients value fresh juice as well as grinding, which appeals to many households. Where idli and dosa batter is made at home every week, wet grinding performance tends to matter more.
