Opalware, Glass and Crockery: What to Compare

Dinnerware comes in several materials, and each suits a different need. An opalware dinner set from La Opala or Cello is lightweight, chip-resistant and microwave-safe, making it the most popular choice for families. A glass dinner set from Luminarc offers a modern, clean look and is often dishwasher-safe.

When comparing, check the number of pieces, what the set includes, such as plates, bowls, serving dishes and spoons, and whether it suits a family of four or six. Pattern and colour also matter; neutral designs work best for large groups with varied tastes.

For smaller budgets, a set of bowls, glasses or serving pieces still feels festive and substantial, and fits more easily into larger programmes.

Opalware is lighter, chip-resistant and microwave-safe, making it practical for daily family use. Glass sets offer a modern look and are often dishwasher-safe. Both make good gifts; we can suggest designs from La Opala, Luminarc and Cello.
