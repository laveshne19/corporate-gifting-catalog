When Gift Cards Make Sense

Gift vouchers work particularly well when your recipients are diverse and a single product will not suit everyone. They are also useful for remote teams, where shipping physical items to many addresses is harder, and for last-minute recognition moments.

That said, a voucher on its own can feel transactional. Many companies find the best results come from pairing a physical gift with a card: a Cello flask set or boAt earbuds plus a small voucher, for example. The physical item creates a memorable moment, while the card adds choice. Gift cards for employees are especially effective in spot awards, referral bonuses and quarterly recognition, where speed and flexibility matter more than presentation.
