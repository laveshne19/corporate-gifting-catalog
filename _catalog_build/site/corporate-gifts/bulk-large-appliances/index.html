Rewards That Anchor Dealer Schemes

Large appliances are the most aspirational rewards in many channel programmes. For consumer durables, FMCG, agri-input and building materials companies, home appliances for dealer incentive schemes create clear, motivating tiers: a microwave or air purifier for the first slab, a refrigerator or washing machine for the next, and a large LED TV or air conditioner for top performers.

Because these rewards represent significant value, documentation is critical. We provide the verified MRP of every model and bill on a GST invoice, which simplifies scheme accounting and audit. We can also help design slabs by suggesting models at each level, based on current availability from our authorised brands. Where schemes run over several months, rewards can be supplied in phases as dealers qualify.

What to Compare Before You Order

Each category has specifications that drive value. For TVs, compare screen size, resolution, smart features and sound output. For a refrigerator bulk purchase, look at capacity in litres, single or double door, energy rating and compressor type. For a washing machine bulk order, the key choices are front or top load, fully or semi-automatic, and drum capacity. For an air conditioner bulk purchase, tonnage, star rating and inverter technology matter most, along with installation needs.

Smaller categories follow similar logic. A microwave oven corporate gift can be solo, grill or convection; a water heater bulk order depends on capacity and whether instant or storage type suits the recipients; an air purifier corporate gift should match the room size. We list these specifications against MRP for each shortlisted model, so your team can compare fairly.
