Business Profiles of Amritsar and Jalandhar

The two cities have distinct economies. Amritsar is a major tourism and hospitality centre, with hotels, travel businesses, textile trading and food processing. Hospitality businesses need gifts for staff, partners and guests, often practical and presentable, such as Cello bottles, Welspun towel sets and kitchen appliances.

Corporate gifts Jalandhar companies source often go to workforces in sports goods, hand tools, leather and rubber goods manufacturing, as well as education institutions. Factory teams value fans, coolers, lunch boxes and appliances, while exporters and senior staff appreciate premium items such as Titan watches or Samsung devices.

Both cities have strong trading and dealer networks, where channel rewards like appliances, smartphones and luggage work well as slab gifts.
