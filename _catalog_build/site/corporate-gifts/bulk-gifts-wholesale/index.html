What Works Best When You Order Gifts in Bulk

Large orders reward products that suit almost everyone. Audio from boAt, Noise or Portronics, Cello bottles and lunch boxes, Prestige or Bajaj kitchen appliances, Titan and Fastrack watches, and VIP or Skybags backpacks all travel well across age groups and locations. For a mixed workforce, many buyers split one budget into two or three product options so staff can pick what they prefer.

When shortlisting bulk gift items, check three things: whether stock is available in the full quantity, whether the product ships safely in its retail box, and whether a matching lower-priced alternative exists if the budget moves. We flag all three in the proposal so procurement is not surprised later. Minimum order quantities vary by product; many start around 25-50 units, and we tell you upfront where a model needs more.

How Our B2B Gifting Process Runs, Step by Step

Our ordering workflow is deliberately simple. You share the occasion, headcount, per-head budget and delivery locations on WhatsApp at +91-9115513366 or by email to info@nalandaenterprises.com. We reply with a curated shortlist and then a formal proposal listing each product, its MRP and your quoted rate.

Once you approve the proposal and issue a purchase order, we confirm stock, pack and dispatch from Chandigarh. A GST invoice goes with the consignment, which keeps your finance team's input credit and audit trail clean. In-stock items typically move within a few working days; branded or customised orders take longer, and we share a realistic timeline before you commit.

Because we have handled 500+ corporate clients and delivered more than 50,000 gifts, the paperwork side, from vendor registration forms to PO references on invoices, is routine for us.

Understanding Wholesale Pricing on Branded Gifts

Wholesale corporate gifts are priced against the product's MRP, not an arbitrary list. The rate you receive depends on quantity, the specific model and current brand schemes, which is why we quote per order rather than publishing a fixed price sheet.

A few practical ways to get better value on bulk gift items wholesale price: consolidate several occasions into one order, keep a flexible alternate model in the brief, and share dispatch addresses early so freight can be planned in one go. Larger single-location deliveries usually cost less per unit to ship than many small drops.

If you need logo branding or custom packaging, it is available on many products, on request, and we will confirm feasibility for the exact model before quoting. Gift cards from 20+ e-gift card brands, including Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra, can be added to the same order.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum quantity for bulk gift orders?

Minimum quantities vary by product and brand. Many items start around 25-50 units, while some appliances or premium electronics may have different thresholds. Share your headcount and we will point out any model where the MOQ does not fit your plan.

How much below MRP are your wholesale rates?

It depends on the model, the quantity and any active brand schemes. Every product in our catalogue has a verified MRP, and we quote a dealer rate below it once we know your numbers. Send the brief on WhatsApp for a written quote.

Can you deliver one bulk order to several offices?

Yes. We dispatch pan-India from Chandigarh and can split a single order across multiple branch or plant addresses. Share the address list with quantities per location when you request the proposal so freight and packing are planned correctly.

Do you provide a GST invoice for bulk purchases?

Every order is billed on a proper GST invoice from Nalanda Enterprises, with your company name, GSTIN and PO reference where required. This allows your finance team to claim eligible input tax credit and keeps vendor records clean.
