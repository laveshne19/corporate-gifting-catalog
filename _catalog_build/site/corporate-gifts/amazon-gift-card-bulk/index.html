When Gift Cards Beat a Physical Gift

Gift cards shine when choice matters more than presentation. For remote or field teams spread across many cities, an Amazon gift card for employees removes the logistics of shipping physical products. For diverse workforces, it avoids the risk of someone receiving an item they already own.

An Amazon gift voucher also works well for instant recognition, such as spot awards, contest winners, survey participation and referral bonuses, where speed matters more than packaging.

Many companies combine the two approaches: a physical gift for festive occasions and a voucher for quick, frequent rewards. Others add a small voucher to a hamper as a flexible extra. We can structure both within a single order and proposal.
