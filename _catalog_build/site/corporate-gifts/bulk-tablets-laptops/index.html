Specs to Compare Before You Buy

Tablets vary more than most gift categories, so it pays to compare. Look at screen size (8 to 11 inches is the usual range), display resolution, RAM and storage, battery capacity, and whether the model supports LTE or is Wi-Fi only. For field teams using tablets on the move, LTE support is often worth the extra cost.

Also consider software support, stylus compatibility and available cases or keyboards. When a tablet as corporate gift is for personal use, a larger screen and good speakers matter more. When it doubles as a work tool, RAM, storage and security features take priority. We can prepare a side-by-side comparison of two or three models from Samsung, Lenovo and others within your budget, so the decision is based on the real use case.
