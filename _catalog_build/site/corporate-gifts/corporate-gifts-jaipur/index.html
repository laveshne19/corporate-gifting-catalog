Gifting for Jaipur's Businesses

Jaipur's hotels and hospitality businesses employ large teams who appreciate practical household gifts, such as Prestige cookware, Cello casseroles, Welspun towels and Bajaj appliances. Gems, jewellery and handicraft exporters often gift to overseas buyers and domestic partners, where premium items like Titan watches, Marshall speakers or VIP luggage make a polished impression.

The city's IT and BPO companies, banks and educational institutions respond well to gadgets: earbuds, smartwatches, power banks and laptop backpacks. Because Rajasthan has hot summers, fans and coolers from Crompton, Havells or Symphony are also welcome gifts for plant and field staff, subject to availability.
