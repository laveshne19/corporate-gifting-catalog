What to Look For in a Corporate Gifting Partner

Most buyers shortlisting corporate gift suppliers compare three things: product range, pricing transparency and reliability at scale. A partner with a narrow catalogue forces you into generic items, while one without verified pricing makes it hard to justify a purchase order internally.

Our catalogue spans audio, wearables, power banks, kitchen and home appliances, dinnerware, luggage, watches, home textiles and fitness products. Every listing carries a verified MRP, so finance teams can see the value of what employees or clients receive, and bulk pricing below MRP is shared on request based on quantity. Because the range sits under one roof, a single order can combine a boAt speaker for the sales team, a Prestige cookware set for long-serving staff and a Cello flask set for plant workers, all on one proposal and one invoice.

How Ordering Works with Us

Buying through a corporate gifting agency should not feel like running a tender every time. Our process is deliberately short. You message your requirement, budget per head and approximate quantity on WhatsApp at +91-9115513366. We reply with a curated proposal of options that fit, including bulk rates and availability.

Once you confirm, we raise the order, arrange logo branding or custom packaging where the product allows it, and dispatch from Chandigarh with a proper GST invoice. In-stock items typically move within a few working days; branded or customised orders take longer, and we tell you the expected window upfront. MOQs vary by product, though many items start around 25-50 units. For teams used to juggling several corporate gift vendors, having one accountable supplier for sourcing, invoicing and dispatch saves real admin time.

Programmes We Support Across Industries

Our corporate gifting services are built around recurring programmes rather than one-off purchases. We regularly handle employee onboarding kits, rewards and recognition, Diwali and festival programmes, client appreciation gifts branded to the company, channel partner and dealer rewards, and sales incentive schemes.

Each industry tends to need something different. Pharma and healthcare firms often gift field teams and doctors practical electronics; FMCG and manufacturing companies reward distributors and shop-floor staff; IT and BFSI teams lean towards wearables, audio and work-from-home gear; education, hospitality and retail buyers usually prefer home and kitchen items with broad appeal. We put together budget-tiered curation for each, so one programme can have a Rs. 500 tier, a Rs. 2,000 tier and a premium tier, all sourced from the same authorised catalogue.
