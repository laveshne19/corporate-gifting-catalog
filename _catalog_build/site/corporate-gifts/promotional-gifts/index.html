Choosing Items That Get Used

The best promotional products are the ones a customer reaches for every day. A Cello water bottle sits on a desk for months; a Portronics charging cable travels in a bag; a boAt neckband goes on every commute. Each of those moments is a quiet brand impression.

When planning, think about who receives the gift and where they will use it. Trade-show visitors appreciate compact, portable items. Retail customers respond to kitchen and home pieces. Corporate buyers value tech accessories. For giveaway items for promotion at scale, lighter products also keep courier costs sensible. Tell us the audience and the campaign goal, and we will shortlist items across categories so you can compare options side by side before committing to a quantity.

Logo Branding and Bulk Ordering

Promotional items with logo branding are available on many products in our catalogue, on request. Bottles, flasks, backpacks, power banks and some tech accessories are the usual candidates, though the method and minimum quantity vary by item, so we confirm feasibility during the proposal stage.

The ordering flow is straightforward. Send your requirement on WhatsApp, and we respond with a proposal showing products, verified MRPs, bulk pricing and branding options. After you approve the artwork and quantities, we place the order and dispatch from Chandigarh with a GST invoice. MOQs vary by product, with many starting around 25-50 units. In-stock plain items typically ship in a few working days; branded or customised orders need longer, and we share a realistic timeline before you confirm.

Budget Tiers for Campaigns

Marketing budgets rarely allow one price point for everyone. We usually structure promotional corporate gifts in tiers. Entry-level items such as cables, basic earphones and Cello bottles suit large walk-in audiences and contests. Mid-range pieces like power banks, Bluetooth speakers and insulated flasks work well for key customers and channel partners. Premium picks from brands like Noise, Titan or Swiss Military are kept for top accounts or lucky-draw winners.

If you are specifically hunting for promotional gift items under 100 rupees, be aware that branded products at that level are limited; we will tell you honestly what is feasible and suggest the nearest useful alternative. For marketing gifts aimed at retaining existing customers, a slightly higher per-unit spend on a genuine branded product usually delivers better recall than a larger quantity of unbranded giveaways.

Frequently asked questions

Which products are most popular for promotions?

Water bottles and flasks, charging cables, power banks, earphones, backpacks and small kitchen items are consistently popular because recipients use them daily. We recommend choosing based on your audience and event format; we can suggest a shortlist once you share the campaign details.

Can every product carry our logo?

No. Logo branding is available on many products, on request, but not on all of them. Some brands and product surfaces do not allow it. We confirm branding feasibility, method and any minimum quantity for each item during the proposal stage.

Do you work as a promotional gifts supplier for events outside Chandigarh?

Yes. We are based in Chandigarh and deliver pan-India. Event kits and campaign stock are dispatched to your office or venue with a GST invoice. In-stock items typically take a few working days; branded orders take longer, so plan ahead of the event date.

What quantity do we need to order?

MOQs vary by product, and many items start around 25-50 units. Branded or customised orders may need higher quantities. Share your campaign size and we will suggest items where your quantity works comfortably, along with bulk pricing below MRP.
