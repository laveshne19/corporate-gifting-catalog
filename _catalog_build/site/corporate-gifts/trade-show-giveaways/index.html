Booth Giveaways That People Actually Keep

The best handouts are small, useful and easy to carry. Unbranded trinkets are forgotten quickly, whereas recognisable products tend to get more attention: Cello bottles, Portronics or Lifelong cables, pocket power banks and entry-level earbuds.

For exhibition giveaways aimed at trade buyers, consider slightly higher-value items given only after a meaningful conversation or a scanned lead. A Bluetooth speaker or smartwatch works well as a qualifying gift.

If you need low-cost items in large numbers, keep one low-cost item for every visitor and a second, better item for qualified leads. This two-tier approach keeps costs in check and rewards the conversations that matter. Logo printing is available on many products, on request, and we confirm feasibility per item.

Quiz Rewards and Lucky Draws

Contests and lucky draws create energy around a booth or launch. Contest prizes can range from earbuds and speakers for quick quizzes to smartphones, tablets or Bose and Marshall audio for the main draw. For lucky draw gifts for customers at dealer meets, kitchen appliances, Symphony coolers and Samsung or Whirlpool home appliances are often more valued than gadgets.

Display the prizes at the booth; seeing a real product draws more participation than a poster alone. We supply products in their retail packaging, so they look authentic on display.

For product launch gifts, match the gift to your brand story. A tech launch might give premium chargers; a home brand could give kitchenware. Launch event giveaways work best when the gift feels connected to what is being launched.

Planning Quantities and Event Timelines

Event deadlines are fixed, so plan backwards from the show date. Share your event date, expected footfall, budget and delivery address, such as your office or the venue, on WhatsApp at +91-9115513366. We send a shortlist and then a proposal with MRPs and quoted rates.

After approval and PO, we dispatch from Chandigarh with a GST invoice. In-stock items typically ship within a few working days; branded or customised items, including logo-printed giveaways, take longer, so allow extra time before the event.

MOQs are product-specific, and a large share of the range starts around 25-50 units. For roadshows that need expo giveaways India-wide, we can split dispatch across venues. It is usually better to over-order the low-cost item slightly and hold the premium prizes to exact quantities.

Frequently asked questions

What are good giveaway items for customers at an expo?

Cables, compact chargers, bottles and pocket power banks are reliable choices because they are useful and portable. For qualified leads or dealers, earbuds, speakers or smartwatches create a stronger impression. We can suggest items at each budget level.

Can giveaways carry our logo?

Logo branding is available on many products, on request, including bottles, bags and some tech accessories. It depends on the product and quantity, and branded items take longer, so share your artwork and deadline early.

How many giveaways should we order for a trade show?

A common approach is one low-cost item for expected visitors and a smaller quantity of better items for qualified leads. Share your expected footfall and we will help estimate quantities, keeping MOQs in mind.

Can you deliver directly to the exhibition venue?

Yes, we can dispatch from Chandigarh to your office or the venue, subject to the venue's receiving rules. Share the venue address, contact person and delivery window, and we will plan the shipment accordingly.
