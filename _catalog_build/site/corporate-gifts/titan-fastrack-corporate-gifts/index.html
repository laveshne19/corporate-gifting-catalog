Titan Versus Fastrack: Picking the Right Line

Titan and Fastrack serve different audiences. Titan's analog collections, with metal or leather straps and classic dials, suit senior managers, long-service awardees and clients who value a traditional, formal watch. Premium Titan ranges step up in finish and presentation for milestone awards.

Fastrack corporate gifts suit younger teams, interns and campus hires, with bold designs, sporty straps and lower budgets. Fastrack also offers smartwatches for recipients who prefer fitness tracking and notifications.

Many companies use both brands in one programme: Fastrack for early-career recognition and Titan for senior or long-tenure awards. This creates a clear, aspirational progression. We share current collections and specifications in the proposal, as designs refresh regularly.

Watches for Tenure, Leadership and Clients

Service anniversaries are the most common reason for a Titan corporate order. Five-year awards often feature Fastrack or entry Titan models, while ten, fifteen and twenty-year milestones warrant premium Titan collections presented in original boxes with a message card from leadership.

For client gifting, a Titan watch communicates quality and long-term commitment. Couple watch sets are another option for recognising employees alongside their spouses on significant milestones.

Engraving or personalisation is available on some models, on request, depending on the case back and brand policy. We confirm feasibility before you approve. Presentation in original Titan packaging, rather than custom boxes, is usually preferred because it reinforces authenticity. A short handover at a team meeting adds to the moment far more than elaborate wrapping.

Frequently asked questions

Which Titan watch suits a long-service award?

For ten years or more, premium Titan analog collections with metal or leather straps are popular. For five years, entry Titan or Fastrack models work well. We suggest options by milestone and budget in the proposal.

Can Titan watches be engraved for awards?

Some models can be engraved on request, subject to the case back and the brand's policy. We confirm feasibility for your shortlisted models before quoting. A personalised message card is another simple option.

Are Titan and Fastrack watches covered by warranty?

Yes. We supply them in original packaging from authorised brand channels, so standard manufacturer warranty applies according to each brand's policy. Warranty details and service information can be included in the proposal.

Can we order a few watches each quarter?

Yes. Many clients run recurring award orders. We can set up a standing proposal with chosen models and MRPs, then process each quarter's list as it comes, subject to model availability.
