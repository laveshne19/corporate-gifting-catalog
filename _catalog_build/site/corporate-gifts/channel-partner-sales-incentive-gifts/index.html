Rewards That Motivate Sales Teams and Partners

Motivation depends on the reward being worth the effort. For gifts for sales team contests, smartphones from Samsung, Motorola, Realme or POCO, smartwatches from Noise or Fire-Boltt, and premium audio from boAt, Marshall or Sennheiser tend to spark real interest. Sales contest prizes for top ranks can step up to Bose speakers, Lenovo tablets or Safari luggage sets.

For dealers and retailers, practical rewards often work better. Whirlpool, Haier and Samsung appliances, Crompton or Usha fans, Symphony coolers and Prestige kitchen products are all popular because they benefit the dealer's household or shop.

Target achievement gifts should be announced with the actual product image and MRP visible. That transparency helps participants understand the value and keeps your scheme credible.

Structuring Dealer Slabs and Loyalty Catalogues

Channel incentive gifts are easiest to manage when grouped into slabs. For example, a dealer achieving a base target might receive a mixer grinder, the next slab a cooler, and the top slab a refrigerator or smartphone. We help build a reward catalogue for loyalty program schemes with each item's MRP listed, so your team can set points or targets against real values.

The same approach works for channel loyalty rewards across a financial year, where partners redeem at intervals. Products can be updated as models change, and we flag replacements before stock runs out.

For frontline and logistics teams, gifts for delivery executives should be sturdy and practical: power banks, rain-ready backpacks, bottles and wired or wireless earbuds all get heavy daily use.

From Scheme Launch to Final Dispatch

Once your scheme design is ready, share the slabs, expected winners and timelines on WhatsApp at +91-9115513366. We prepare a proposal with MRPs and quoted rates for each reward, which you can use in scheme communications.

At closure, send the winner list with addresses. We dispatch from Chandigarh pan-India with a GST invoice, whether rewards go to your offices, regional depots or directly to dealers. There is no single MOQ: it varies by model, and many sit around 25-50 units. Frequently asked questions

What are good scheme gifts for dealers?

Dealers often prefer household appliances such as coolers, fans, mixer grinders, microwave ovens and refrigerators, along with smartphones. We can suggest options per slab with verified MRPs so your scheme values are clear and consistent.

Can you supply gifts for partners across many states?

Yes. We dispatch pan-India from Chandigarh to dealer addresses, regional offices or depots in any state. Share the final winner list with addresses and quantities, and we will plan dispatch and invoicing accordingly.

Can rewards be priced for a points-based programme?

We provide MRPs for every item and quote your purchase rate below it. Many clients use MRP as the reference to assign points, then track redemptions internally. We can refresh the list when models change.

Do incentive gifts come with a GST invoice?

Every dispatch is billed on a GST invoice from Nalanda Enterprises. If your scheme needs separate invoices by region or business unit, tell us at the proposal stage and we will organise billing accordingly.
