Choosing the Right Speaker for the Audience

Speakers come in very different formats, and choosing the right one depends on who receives it. A mini bluetooth speaker bulk order suits large teams and onboarding kits, as compact models are easy to pack and carry. Mid-size portable speakers with better battery life and water resistance work for R&R awards and festive gifts.

A party speaker for gifting, with bigger drivers and lighting effects, is popular with younger teams and as a lucky draw prize. For senior clients, Marshall and Bose speakers deliver a premium feel and design that suits a home or office.

When comparing, check output wattage, battery hours, water resistance rating and connectivity options such as auxiliary input or TWS pairing. We summarise these for each shortlisted model in our proposal.
