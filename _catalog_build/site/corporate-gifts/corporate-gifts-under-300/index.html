What to Look For at This Budget

At lower budgets, brand recognition and daily usefulness matter more than novelty. Categories where many models fit this band include data cables, compact chargers, mugs, basic bottles, lunch box accessories and small desk items from known brands like Cello, Portronics, Lifelong and DailyObjects.

When reviewing gift items under 300, check the build quality and packaging, since a low-cost item in a flimsy pack looks cheaper than it is. A branded retail box instantly lifts the perceived value.

If your budget is tighter still, there are options for corporate gifts under 200 and corporate gifts under 250, typically cables, mugs and small accessories. For event handouts or large factory workforces, even gifts under 100 for employees can be arranged in some categories, subject to current stock and MRPs.

Where Low-Budget Gifts Work Best

Low-budget gifts fit best where reach matters more than individual value. Large-scale festive programmes for plant workers, contract staff or delivery partners, conference kits for hundreds of attendees, and retailer or customer loyalty handouts are typical uses.

Diwali gifts under 200 are a common requirement for large workforces. Useful choices include steel tumblers, small serving bowls, mugs or basic flasks, which carry a festive feel without stretching the budget.

Combining two small items can also add value. A cable paired with a mug, or a bottle with a lunch pouch, feels more generous than either alone. We can quote such combinations and pack them together, which saves your team time on the day of distribution.
