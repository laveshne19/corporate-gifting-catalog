The Usha Range for Corporate Programmes

Usha's catalogue covers several practical categories. Ceiling, table and pedestal fans are staples for summer programmes and worker gifting. Kitchen appliances such as mixer grinders, electric kettles, sandwich makers, OTGs and induction cooktops suit festive gifts for families.

Dry and steam irons make practical everyday gifts, while room heaters are well suited to winter programmes in North India.

When comparing Usha models, check wattage, capacity and warranty terms, and match the category to the season. We share current models and specs in the proposal so HR and procurement can compare options quickly.

A simple approach for mixed workforces is to offer two Usha products at a similar value, such as a fan and a mixer grinder, and let employees choose. It respects different household needs while keeping one brand, one proposal and one invoice. Gifts built this way also avoid duplication in homes that already own a particular appliance.

Where Usha Gifts Fit Best
