What Goes into a Good WFH Kit

Start with the items that affect daily work. Audio comes first: a good headset or TWS earbuds make calls clearer and reduce fatigue. A laptop stand gift improves posture and is one of the most appreciated items in remote setups. A reliable power bank or multi-port charger from Portronics, Belkin or WiWu helps during outages.

After that, add comfort and wellbeing items: a Cello insulated bottle or tumbler, a desk lamp, or a fitness accessory. For a fuller wfh essentials kit, some companies include a webcam, a keyboard-mouse combo or a backpack for days in the office. We suggest choosing four to six items around a clear theme, rather than filling the box with low-value extras that end up unused.

Delivery to Employee Homes

The practical challenge with gifts for remote employees is logistics. Collecting addresses, handling different cities and keeping track of deliveries can overwhelm an HR team. We simplify it: share a spreadsheet with names, addresses and phone numbers, and we plan dispatch from Chandigarh.

The order flow is the same as every programme we run. You send the brief and budget on WhatsApp, we return a proposal with verified MRPs, bulk pricing below MRP and stock position, you confirm, and we dispatch with a GST invoice. In-stock kits typically reach homes within a few working days; kits with custom packaging or logo branding, available on many products on request, take longer. MOQs vary by product, though many items start around 25-50 units, which suits most team sizes.

Kits for Hybrid and Onboarding Use

Remote team gifts India companies send are not just for fully remote staff. Hybrid work gift ideas often combine home and commute needs: a laptop backpack, a neckband for travel, a power bank and a tumbler that works at both desks.

WFH kits also double as onboarding kits. New joiners who start remotely appreciate receiving their essentials in the first week, and a consistent kit gives them the same first impression as office-based colleagues. We can set up a standard kit and fulfil it in batches as new employees join. For recognition moments, a smaller top-up kit with one premium item, such as a Noise smartwatch or Sennheiser headset, works well as a reward for gifts for remote workers who have delivered strong results.
