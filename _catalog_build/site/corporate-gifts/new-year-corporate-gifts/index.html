Gifts That Carry Into the Year

A new year gift should be something people use from the first week of January. Desk and commute essentials are ideal: wireless earbuds from boAt or Noise, Portronics power banks, Belkin or WiWu chargers, insulated Cello bottles and smart Skybags backpacks. New year tech gifts such as smartwatches or Bluetooth speakers also work well for teams returning from holidays.

For offices that like a fresh start theme, fitness gear and bottles tie in with resolutions, while Philips or Havells grooming and personal care products suit an individual gift.

If you want new year gift ideas for office celebrations, consider matching items for the whole team. A shared gadget, given together at a kick-off meeting, sets a collective tone for the year ahead.

Gifts for Clients and Customers at Year Start

Sending new year gifts for clients keeps your relationship visible after the festive rush has passed. A single well-chosen product, such as a Titan watch, a Marshall speaker or a Sennheiser headset, stands out in January when fewer gifts arrive.

For retailers and consumer brands, small thank-yous for loyal buyers can be lighter but still branded, such as bottles, mugs or cables. Logo branding is available on many products, on request, and we confirm feasibility per item.

New year gift hampers are another good choice for key accounts. A hamper might combine a tech hero product with a desk accessory and a gift card from Amazon, Flipkart or Myntra, presented with a message from your leadership team. We can build tiered hamper options for different client groups.

Frequently asked questions

What are good new year gifts for employees?

Earbuds, smartwatches, power banks, bottles and backpacks are popular because they are used daily through the year. For a more personal touch, grooming kits or fitness accessories fit the fresh-start theme. We can suggest options at your per-head budget.

Do you have a new year gift under 500 rupees?

Yes, several categories have models in that band, including bottles, cables, mugs and compact accessories. Exact options depend on current stock and MRPs, so share your quantity and we will recommend suitable items with verified pricing.

When should we order new year gifts?

We recommend placing orders by early December, since offices close and logistics slow in the last week of the year. Branded or customised gifts need extra time, so earlier is better for those.

Can you send a new year gift for staff at home?

Yes. We can dispatch from Chandigarh directly to employee home addresses across India, billed on one GST invoice to the company. Share names, addresses and phone numbers in a simple sheet.
