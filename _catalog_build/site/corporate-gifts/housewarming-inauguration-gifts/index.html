Gift Ideas for Openings and Launches

For a new office opening gift that guests will remember, choose something that works at home as well as at work. Table clocks and watches from Titan, compact Bluetooth speakers, Philips or Havells kettles, and La Opala serveware all feel appropriate for business guests without being overly personal.

Showroom inauguration gifts often go to dealers, walk-in customers and local dignitaries, so it helps to create two tiers: a modest token for general guests and a more substantial gift for key partners. Shop opening gift ideas that work well at scale include Cello flask sets, dinnerware pieces and insulated bottles. When planning gifts for new office opening client visits, a premium desk item or a small appliance usually strikes the right balance between courtesy and value.

Housewarming and New Home Gifts

Housewarming is one of the few occasions where practical home items are exactly what people want. Dinner sets from La Opala or Luminarc, Prestige cookware, Welspun bed linen and small kitchen appliances from Philips, Bajaj or Usha are safe, welcome choices.

For griha pravesh gifts, many buyers prefer something for the kitchen or pooja space, such as a quality dinner set or a serving collection. When a housewarming gift for colleague is being organised by a team, pooling a budget towards a single good appliance is often better than several small items. Housewarming gifts under 2000 rupees might include a Cello flask set, a Philips kettle or a set of La Opala bowls; we can shortlist options in your range from verified MRPs.

Timing, Packaging and Ordering

Inaugurations run to a fixed date, so timing matters more than usual. We suggest sharing your guest count and budget at least two weeks ahead, especially if you want custom packaging or a message card, which is available on many products on request.

The process is simple. Send the brief on WhatsApp, receive a proposal with options, bulk pricing below MRP and availability, confirm the order, and we dispatch from Chandigarh with a GST invoice. In-stock items typically ship within a few working days; customised orders take longer. MOQs vary by product, with many items starting around 25-50 units, though smaller mixed orders for VIP guests can also be arranged. We can deliver directly to the venue so gifts are ready on the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for guests at an office opening?

Useful items with broad appeal work best: Titan clocks, small kitchen appliances, La Opala serveware, Cello flask sets or compact speakers. We recommend a standard gift for all guests and a premium option for key clients or partners.

Can the gifts carry our company name?

Custom packaging, message cards or logo branding are available on many products, on request. Branding adds some lead time, so share your inauguration date early and we will confirm what is feasible for each product in the proposal.

Can you deliver gifts to the inauguration venue?

Yes. We dispatch from Chandigarh to venues across India. In-stock items typically take a few working days, and customised orders longer. Share the venue address and event date so we can plan dispatch with a comfortable buffer.

Do you have options for small guest lists?

Yes. While many products start around 25-50 units for bulk pricing, we also help with smaller mixed orders for VIP guests or senior partners. Share the guest breakdown and we will suggest suitable combinations.
