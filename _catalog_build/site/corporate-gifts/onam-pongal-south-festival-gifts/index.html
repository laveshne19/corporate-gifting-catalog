Gifts That Suit Harvest Festivals

Most regional festivals in India are harvest celebrations centred on family meals, which makes kitchen and home products a natural fit. Onam corporate gifts often include dinnerware for the Sadya, Prestige or Preethi kitchen appliances, and brass-look serveware.

Pongal gifts work well around the idea of the new pot and the family kitchen: a Prestige pressure cooker, a Sujata or Preethi mixer grinder, or a Cello storage set. A pongal gift hamper might combine a cookware piece with a La Opala bowl set. For Ugadi gifts, similar kitchen and home products suit Karnataka and Telugu-speaking teams. Choosing brands that are familiar and trusted in the region, such as Preethi in Tamil Nadu, adds to the gift's appeal.

Sankranti, Lohri and Baisakhi

January brings several festivals at once. Sankranti gifting in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra, Pongal gifts for employees in Tamil Nadu, and Lohri corporate gifts in Punjab and Haryana all fall within the same week. Companies with national footprints can run one January programme and localise the message card for each region.

Being based in Chandigarh, we know the northern calendar well. Lohri gifts tend to be warm and practical, such as Cello flasks, Havells kettles or Welspun blankets and bed linen. Baisakhi gifts in April suit Punjab and Haryana teams, and home appliances or dinnerware are popular choices. A single product can often work across festivals, with only the card and timing changed for each office.

Running Region-Wise Festival Programmes

Managing festival gifting for multiple states can overwhelm a central HR team. We simplify it by creating a festival calendar with you, matching products and budgets to each region, and dispatching to each office ahead of its festival date.

The process follows our standard flow: brief us on WhatsApp, receive a proposal with verified MRPs and bulk pricing below MRP, confirm, and we dispatch from Chandigarh with a GST invoice. In-stock items typically reach offices within a few working days; branded or customised orders take longer, so allow extra time for southern locations during peak season. Each item has its own MOQ, commonly somewhere around 25-50 units. For Onam gifts for employees in Kerala, briefing us three weeks ahead gives a comfortable buffer.

Frequently asked questions

What are popular Onam gifts for corporate teams?

Dinnerware sets for the Onam Sadya, Preethi or Prestige kitchen appliances, cookware and Cello storage sets are popular. They reflect the family meal at the heart of Onam and are useful long after the festival.

Can we run Pongal and Sankranti gifting from one order?

Yes. Both fall in mid-January, and one product range often suits both. We can prepare a single proposal, split dispatch by office and change message cards per region, all on one GST invoice.

Do you deliver festival gifts to South Indian cities?

Yes. We dispatch from Chandigarh to Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi and other cities. In-stock items typically take a few working days; branded orders take longer, so we recommend early briefing.

Which brands work well for regional festival gifting?

Preethi and Sujata mixer grinders, Prestige cookware, La Opala and Luminarc dinnerware, Cello storage and Welspun textiles are all reliable choices. They are among the 54 authorised brands in our catalogue.
