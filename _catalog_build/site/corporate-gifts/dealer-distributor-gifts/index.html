Rewarding Your Channel by Slab

Channel schemes work best when rewards scale clearly with performance. We usually help clients structure three to five slabs. Entry-level gifts for retailers who meet a basic target might be a Cello flask set, a Prestige pressure cooker or a Bajaj iron. Mid-tier partners could receive a Havells fan, a Philips mixer or a boAt speaker.

Top slabs deserve gifts that feel aspirational: a Samsung smartphone, a Lenovo tablet, a Whirlpool or Haier appliance, or a Safari luggage set for a family trip. Because every product has a verified MRP, it is easy to communicate the reward value in scheme leaflets. Gifts for dealers and distributors that the whole household uses tend to generate the strongest goodwill, since the reward is visible at home, not just in the shop.

Dealer Meets and Festival Gifting

Dealer meet gifts need to be ready on a specific date and often go to a hundred or more attendees at once. We plan these orders backwards from the event, confirm stock early, and dispatch to the venue with a GST invoice. Distributor meet gifts can be split into a common welcome gift for everyone and a separate award gift for top performers presented on stage.

Festivals are the other big moment. Gifts for dealers on Diwali are expected in most trades, and demand for popular items rises sharply in the weeks before. We recommend locking your order early. Our process is the same throughout: share the brief on WhatsApp, receive a proposal with bulk pricing below MRP, confirm, and we dispatch from Chandigarh. MOQs vary by product, with many items starting around 25-50 units.

Rewarding Influencers and Tradespeople

Not every channel partner sits behind a counter. Building materials, electrical, paint and automotive brands often reward influencers who recommend products on site. Gifts for contractors and site supervisors tend to be practical: power banks, rugged backpacks, Bluetooth speakers and smartwatches.

For gifts for electricians and plumbers, tool-free items that survive daily use are ideal, such as neckband earphones, insulated bottles and phone accessories. Gifts for mechanics in automotive aftermarket schemes follow a similar pattern, with household appliances popular at higher slabs. A gift for shopkeepers running small kirana or pharmacy counters is often a kitchen or home item that benefits the family. Logo branding is available on many products, on request, which helps reinforce your brand every time the item is used.

Frequently asked questions

What gifts work best for high-performing partners?

Items with strong household value: Samsung smartphones, Lenovo tablets, Havells or Crompton appliances, Whirlpool or Haier home appliances and Safari luggage. We usually suggest two or three options per slab so you can match the reward to the scheme's target.

Can you handle a scheme with hundreds of partners in different towns?

Yes. We dispatch from Chandigarh to dealer locations across India with GST invoices. In-stock items typically ship within a few working days, while branded items take longer. We can plan dispatch town-wise or deliver in bulk to your regional office.

Do you provide the MRP for scheme communication?

Yes. Every product in our catalogue has a verified MRP, which you can use in scheme leaflets and announcements. Your purchase price is quoted separately at bulk rates below MRP based on quantity.

How early should we order gifts for a dealer meet?

We suggest at least three to four weeks for large meets, and more if you need logo branding or custom packaging. Share the attendee count and event date, and we will confirm stock and a realistic dispatch window.
