Choosing Dinnerware for Gifting

Dinnerware gifts come in many configurations, so it helps to know what to compare. Look at piece count, which typically ranges from small bowl sets to full dinner sets for six, then check the material (opal glass is lightweight, chip-resistant and microwave-safe in most ranges), pattern and whether the set includes serving bowls and plates.

For employees, a full dinner set suits long-service awards and senior tiers, while bowl sets, dessert sets or mug sets suit entry and mid budgets. Neutral and classic patterns have broader appeal than bold designs in large programmes. The La Opala dinner set price varies considerably by range and piece count; every product carries a verified MRP, and we can shortlist sets across budgets so you can compare value side by side.

La Opala and Luminarc Compared

Both brands offer quality dinnerware, but they suit slightly different briefs. La Opala is widely recognised in Indian households and is often the first name buyers ask for in festive gifting. Its ranges cover everyday sets through to more premium collections.

A luminarc dinner set offers a European design sensibility and can be a good alternative when you want variety across tiers or a different look. Luminarc corporate gifts work well for client appreciation, where a contemporary design helps the gift stand out. Many clients combine both brands in one programme, using one for staff tiers and the other for managers or clients. We can present options from both brands at your budget, with verified MRPs, so you can choose on design and value.
