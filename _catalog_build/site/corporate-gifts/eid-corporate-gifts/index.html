Gifts That Suit Eid Celebrations at Home

Eid centres on family meals and hosting, so gifts for the home are especially welcome. La Opala and Luminarc dinner sets, glass serving bowls, Cello casseroles and Prestige or Bajaj kitchen appliances all fit naturally into festive cooking and serving. Welspun towel and bed linen sets are also appreciated for the home.

For younger recipients, audio and wearables from boAt, Noise or Fire-Boltt are popular, and a Titan watch makes a respected gift for senior colleagues or clients.

An eid gift hamper can combine a serving set with a flask and a message card, creating something that feels festive without being overly elaborate. We avoid consumables and food in our hampers, and focus on durable, branded products that are used well beyond the festival.

Planning Gifts Through Ramadan

Many companies prefer to deliver gifts during the last days of Ramadan so they arrive before Eid. Ramadan gifts such as serveware, dinner sets or a ramzan gift hamper give families time to use them for iftar and festive hosting.

Because Eid dates follow the lunar calendar and can shift by a day, we suggest placing orders two to three weeks before the expected date. That allows time for proposal approval, dispatch from Chandigarh and delivery to your offices.

Eid corporate gifts are often given alongside a note from leadership, which adds warmth. Message cards and logo branding are available on many products, on request, and we confirm feasibility for the items you select.

Frequently asked questions

What are good eid gifts for employees?

Dinner sets, serving bowls, casseroles, kitchen appliances and towel sets are popular because they suit festive hosting. For younger staff, earbuds or smartwatches also work well. We can recommend options at your per-head budget with verified MRPs.

Do your Eid hampers include sweets or dry fruits?

No. We supply durable branded products only, such as serveware, appliances and textiles. Many clients pair our hampers with sweets sourced locally. We can suggest serving pieces that suit dry fruits or sweets.

When should Eid gift orders be placed?

We suggest ordering two to three weeks before the expected Eid date, since lunar dates can shift slightly. Where stock is available, dispatch typically takes a few working days; branding or customisation adds time.

Can Eid gifts be delivered to several offices?

Yes. We dispatch pan-India from Chandigarh and can split one order across multiple offices or client addresses, billed on a GST invoice. Share quantities per location when you send the brief.
