Types of Bottles for Corporate Gifting

Choosing the right bottle starts with how people will carry it. A plain steel water bottle bulk order suits large teams and outdoor staff, as single-wall steel is light and durable. An insulated bottle corporate gift, with double-wall vacuum construction, keeps drinks hot or cold for hours and feels more premium. Sippers with flip lids and straws suit gyms and commutes, and a sipper bottle bulk order is popular for wellness initiatives.

For a more tech-forward option, bottles with a temperature display on the lid are a hit with younger teams, and an LED temperature bottle bulk order is possible subject to current availability. A copper bottle corporate gift appeals to recipients who prefer traditional materials, again depending on stock. We list capacity, material and insulation details against each bottle's MRP, so you can compare like for like.

Customised Water Bottle with Logo

Bottles are among the best products for branding because the surface is large, smooth and visible every time someone takes a sip. Printing or engraving works well on steel and insulated bottles; logo branding is available on many products, on request, and we confirm feasibility for each model before you order.

Consider where the logo will be seen. A single-colour print or laser engraving on a matte finish usually looks more premium than a large multi-colour print. For onboarding kits, matching the bottle colour to your brand palette adds polish. For events, a simple logo and tagline keep costs down across large quantities. Remember that branded orders take longer than in-stock items, which typically ship within a few working days, so plan accordingly for launches and conferences.

Frequently asked questions

Which bottle brands do you supply?

Cello is our main brand for bottles, flasks, sippers and lunch boxes, with options from other authorised brands in our catalogue as well. Each product is listed with its verified MRP, and we recommend models based on use, audience and budget.

Do insulated bottles really keep drinks hot and cold?

Double-wall vacuum insulated bottles are designed to hold temperature for several hours, with exact performance varying by model. We share each model's stated specifications in the proposal, so you can compare insulation, capacity and weight before choosing.

What is the best bottle for an onboarding kit?

An insulated steel bottle of moderate capacity works well, as it suits office desks, commutes and travel. It also takes logo branding cleanly on many models, on request. Pair it with a backpack or desk accessory for a complete kit.

Can we order bottles in multiple colours?

Many models come in several colours, and we can split quantities across them subject to stock. For branded orders, confirm the colour split early, since branding is applied per colour batch and adds to the lead time.
