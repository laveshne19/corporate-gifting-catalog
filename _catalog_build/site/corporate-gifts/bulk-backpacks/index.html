What to Compare in a Laptop Backpack

Backpacks differ more than they appear in photos. Start with the laptop compartment size, since 15.6-inch compatibility covers most corporate laptops. Then compare capacity in litres, padding on the back panel and straps, water resistance, number of compartments and the quality of zips.

An anti theft backpack corporate gift, with hidden zips and a back-facing main compartment, suits employees who commute on public transport or travel frequently. For client-facing staff, a sleeker laptop backpack corporate gift in a neutral colour often looks more professional than a sporty design. We can shortlist three or four models across Skybags, Safari, Swiss Military and DailyObjects within your budget, with specifications laid out side by side, so the choice is based on how your team actually works.

Office Bags, Slings and Branding

Not every recipient needs a full backpack. Corporate laptop bags in a briefcase or messenger style suit senior staff and formal environments. A sling bag corporate gift works for event attendees, field staff who carry only a phone and tablet, or as a secondary gift alongside a flask or power bank.
