Choosing from the Prestige Kitchen Range

Prestige appliances corporate gifting programmes usually centre on a few proven categories. Induction cooktops are compact, useful in homes and hostels alike, and easy to ship in volume. Mixer grinders, electric kettles, sandwich makers and rice cookers suit mid-range budgets. Pressure cookers and cookware sets appeal to families and are often chosen for manufacturing and plant teams, where durability matters most.

When comparing appliances, look at wattage, capacity, safety features and whether cookware is induction-compatible. For induction cooktops specifically, compare power settings, preset menus and panel type. We shortlist models that fit your budget and audience and list each one's verified MRP, so HR and procurement teams can compare options without trawling through the full range.

Gift Combinations by Budget

A prestige gift set can be as simple as a pressure cooker with a matching pan or as generous as a multi-piece cookware collection. Entry-level combinations such as a tawa and kadai suit large workforces, subject to current MRPs. Mid-range options include non-stick cookware sets, pressure cooker combos and electric kettles. At the top end, an induction cooktop with compatible cookware makes a complete, practical kitchen gift.

Prestige gifts work particularly well for Diwali, Durga Puja and Pongal, and as housewarming or wedding gifts for employees. They also suit dealer and channel partner programmes, where a useful home product is appreciated by the whole family. We can build tiered sets so different grades receive a proportionate gift, all within one proposal and one GST invoice.

Many Prestige cookware ranges are induction-compatible, but not all. We state compatibility for each item in the proposal, which matters if you plan to pair cookware with an induction cooktop as a single gift set.
