Popular Picks for Brothers and Sisters

Raksha Bandhan gifts traditionally go both ways, so it helps to cover both. Popular rakhi gifts for sister include Titan or Fastrack watches, Noise or Fire-Boltt smartwatches, Philips personal care appliances and La Opala serving sets. Electronic gifts for sister on rakhi are increasingly common, with wireless earbuds and compact speakers leading the list.

A rakhi gift for brother might be a boAt or Mivi audio product, a Portronics power bank, a Skybags backpack or a smartwatch. For families, kitchen appliances from Prestige or Bajaj make a practical shared gift.

If you are gifting employees rather than their siblings, choose items that work either way: gadgets, bottles and gift cards from Amazon, Flipkart or Myntra let the recipient decide how to use them.

Rakhi Programmes for Employees and Clients

Rakhi corporate gifts are usually smaller in scale than Diwali, which makes them ideal for companies wanting a mid-year touchpoint. Rakhi gifts for employees can be a simple standard item for everyone, or a small choice menu at one budget.

For rakhi gifts for clients, a curated box works well: a Raksha Bandhan gift hamper might combine a premium gadget with a steel bottle and a message from your team. Custom packaging and logo branding are options on many products, on request.

Consumer brands and retailers sometimes use rakhi as a promotional window, offering a gift with purchase. For these, mid-priced audio, watches and kitchen products work best because customers recognise the brand and its value immediately.

Can we buy rakhi gifts in bulk for a promotion?

Yes. We supply branded audio, wearables, watches and kitchen products in volume for gift-with-purchase and dealer promotions. Share expected quantities early so we can check stock and quote dealer rates below MRP.
