Choosing Chargers and Cables That Match Today's Phones

Compatibility is the first check. Most new phones use USB-C, so Type C cable bulk orders suit the majority of employees, while a few iPhone users may still need Lightning. For chargers, check the wattage and fast-charging standard: a fast charger should support common protocols so it works across brands.

For desks and home offices, a 3 in 1 wireless charger bulk order is popular, as it charges a phone, earbuds and a smartwatch together. For teams working on laptops, a multi-port USB hub solves the port shortage on thin notebooks.

Field teams and frequent drivers benefit from a car charger bulk order, especially dual-port models that charge two devices at once. We summarise specifications for each shortlisted product in the proposal, so you can compare easily.
