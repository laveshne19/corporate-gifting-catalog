Festive Timing: Durga Puja Comes First

In Kolkata, the festive calendar starts earlier than in much of India. Durga Puja corporate gifts Kolkata teams receive are often the most important of the year, and many companies distribute them before the Puja holidays begin. That means orders need to be placed well ahead, usually a few weeks before Mahalaya.

Popular Puja gifts include La Opala and Luminarc dinner sets, Cello casseroles, Welspun textiles and kitchen appliances from Prestige and Bajaj, all suited to family celebrations and hosting.

Diwali corporate gifts Kolkata companies send usually follow within weeks, so some buyers plan both occasions in one proposal: a home-focused gift for Puja and a tech or personal gift for Diwali. This keeps approvals simple and pricing consistent.

Kolkata's Industries and What They Gift

Kolkata's economy spans BFSI, IT and services in Salt Lake Sector V and New Town, FMCG distribution, tea, engineering and retail. Each shapes gifting preferences differently.

IT and services teams appreciate earbuds, smartwatches and backpacks. FMCG and distribution businesses often run dealer and channel rewards where appliances, fans and coolers make strong slab gifts. Banks and financial firms prefer premium client gifts such as Titan watches or Philips appliances.
