Practical Picks for a New Home

The most useful wedding gifts are the ones that set up a household. Kitchen appliances as wedding gift choices remain the most popular: a Philips or Havells mixer grinder, a Prestige induction cooktop, a Bajaj OTG or a Sujata juicer mixer. Dinnerware from La Opala or Luminarc is equally welcome and long-lasting.

For a wedding gift for couple recipients who already have the basics, consider Welspun bed linen and towel sets, a Philips air fryer, or luggage from Safari or VIP for the honeymoon. Larger team budgets can stretch to a Whirlpool, Haier or IFB home appliance. We usually recommend asking a close colleague about the couple's existing setup, so the gift fills a gap rather than duplicating something they already have.

Company Policies for Employee Weddings

Many organisations offer a standard wedding gift for employee milestones as part of their benefits. A fixed gift, or a choice of two or three, keeps the policy fair and easy to administer. HR simply shares the employee's details, and we dispatch the gift to the office or home.

A wedding gift for colleague from a department is usually pooled, and our role is to help the team find the best item within the collected amount. Wedding gift under 5000 rupees options include a Prestige cookware set, a Philips mixer grinder, a La Opala dinner set or a Welspun bedding set, depending on current MRPs. Custom packaging or a message card from the team is available on many products, on request.
