What to Put in the Kit

A good employee joining kit covers the basics a new hire will use from day one. Most kits are built around three or four items: an insulated steel bottle or sipper, a laptop backpack, a tech accessory such as earbuds, a power bank or a wireless mouse, and a desk item like a mobile stand or organiser. Adding a Fastrack watch lifts the kit for senior hires without changing its structure.

Think about where people will work. Office-based teams value desk accessories and a good bottle. Hybrid and remote staff benefit from a laptop stand, a BOYA microphone for calls or quality earbuds. Field teams need a sturdy backpack and a reliable power bank. Keeping one core kit and swapping a single item by role is an easy way to tailor it without adding complexity for procurement.

Welcome Kit Ideas for Different Teams and Industries

New employee welcome kit ideas vary by sector. IT and software companies often prefer tech-forward kits with earbuds, a laptop backpack and a wireless mouse. BFSI and consulting firms lean towards a more formal look: a quality backpack, a steel flask and a watch for senior roles. Manufacturing and plant teams appreciate durable kits with a steel bottle, a lunch box and a practical bag for daily travel.

Campus hiring brings its own needs. When a large batch of freshers joins on the same day, consistency and timely delivery matter more than variety, so we suggest a single kit design. For lateral and leadership hires, a smaller premium kit with a Titan watch or Sennheiser headphones reflects the role. A welcome kit for employees with logo branding, where the products allow it, adds a strong sense of identity from the first day.

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical budget for a joining kit?

Budgets vary widely by company and role. Many teams set one standard joining kit for all hires and a richer version for senior roles. Share your per-kit budget and we will propose combinations that fit, with verified MRPs and bulk pricing below MRP for every item.

Can the kit be branded with our company logo?

Logo branding and custom packaging are available on many products, on request, including bottles, backpacks and several desk accessories. We confirm feasibility for each item in your kit before production. Branded orders take longer than in-stock ones, so plan ahead of joining dates.

Can you ship onboarding gifts for new employees to multiple offices?

Yes. We dispatch from Chandigarh to offices across India. Share the addresses and quantities for each location, and we will plan dispatch so every site receives its kits in time for joining dates, with a GST invoice covering the order.

Is a joining kit different from a welcome gift?

Usually, yes. A joining kit is a standard set of useful items for every hire, while a welcome gift for new employee milestones may be a single item, such as a watch or earbuds, sent by a manager. We can supply both, often within the same order.
