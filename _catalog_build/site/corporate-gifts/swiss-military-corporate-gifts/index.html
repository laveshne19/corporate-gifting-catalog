The Swiss Military Range

Swiss Military's catalogue covers several categories that work well in gifting. Luggage and travel gear, including trolley bags, backpacks and duffels, suit travelling professionals. Watches offer a classic, gift-worthy option for milestone awards. Drinkware such as flasks and bottles, along with power banks and tech accessories, cover mid and entry budgets.

A swiss military watch corporate gift is often chosen for long-service awards or client gifts, since a watch carries a sense of occasion. We can shortlist products across categories within your budget, with verified MRPs, to help you compare.

Luggage and Gift Sets

A swiss military trolley bag corporate gift is one of the most impressive options at the upper-mid budget. Cabin trolleys suit frequent flyers, while larger check-in sizes appeal to families. Compare shell material, wheel type, lock mechanism, weight and interior organisation before choosing.

For a Diwali or year-end programme, you can create tiers within the brand: a flask or power bank for staff, a watch for managers and a trolley for leadership, keeping the programme visually consistent.
