Analog, Smart and Paired Watch Options

The right watch depends on the recipient and occasion. An analog watch gift for men from Titan or Swiss Military suits senior managers, long-service awardees and client gifting, with classic designs that work in formal settings. Fastrack offers youthful, sporty styles for younger teams.

For tech-savvy recipients, smartwatches from Noise and Fire-Boltt track fitness, notifications and calls, and often suit R&R programmes.

A watch gift set, pairing a watch with a wallet or accessory, adds presentation value.
