Mivi offers TWS earbuds, neckbands and speakers with clean, understated styling. Mivi corporate gifting appeals to companies that want reliable everyday audio that suits office and commute use alike.

boUlt specialises in earbuds and neckbands with bold designs and long battery life, and its budget-friendly pricing suits large headcounts. Combining these brands lets you offer a smartwatch for one tier and earbuds for another, all within a consistent value range.
