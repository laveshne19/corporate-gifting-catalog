Types of Lunch Boxes and Who They Suit

Lunch boxes come in several formats, each suited to a different routine. Insulated steel lunch boxes keep food warm for hours and suit office staff and commuters. Multi-container tiffins suit employees who carry full meals, especially in plants and field roles.

An electric lunch box corporate gift is useful for staff without access to a microwave, since it reheats food with a simple plug-in. Lunch bags add convenience, and a lunch bag bulk order paired with a bottle makes a tidy onboarding kit.

When comparing options, check the number and capacity of containers, leak-proof lids, insulation duration and ease of cleaning. We share specs for shortlisted models in the proposal, so you can pick what fits your workforce.

Casseroles and Tiffins as Festive Gifts

Casseroles are a festive favourite, especially for families who host meals. A casserole gift set from Cello keeps rotis and curries warm and looks good on the dining table. A casserole set bulk order works well for Diwali and New Year programmes, especially for manufacturing and pharma teams.

A tiffin box corporate gift is a thoughtful everyday alternative, particularly for employees who commute or work long shifts.

For steel lunch box wholesale orders, we can include matching bottles to create a combo, which is popular for onboarding and wellness programmes. Logo branding on lunch boxes and bags is available on many products, on request, and we confirm feasibility per item.

Frequently asked questions

Which lunch box is best for office employees?

Insulated steel lunch boxes with two or three containers are popular for office staff, as they keep food warm and are easy to clean. For employees without microwaves, an electric lunch box is a helpful alternative.

Can lunch boxes be combined with bottles?

Yes. A lunch box and bottle combination is a practical onboarding or wellness gift. We can quote matching Cello products together and pack them as a set for easy distribution.

Can lunch boxes carry our logo?

Logo branding is available on many lunch boxes and bags, on request. Feasibility depends on the material and order size, so we confirm options for your chosen products before quoting.

How quickly can lunch boxes be delivered?

In-stock items typically ship within a few working days of PO confirmation. Branded or customised orders take longer. We dispatch pan-India from Chandigarh and share a realistic timeline in the proposal.
