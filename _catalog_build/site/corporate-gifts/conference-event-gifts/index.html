What to Put in Attendee Kits

A good delegate kit balances usefulness with portability. Attendees are often travelling, so the conference kit should fit into a carry-on. A laptop backpack or sling bag forms the base, and inside you might add a Cello insulated bottle, a Portronics power bank, a charging cable and a notebook.

For shorter seminars or smaller sessions, a single quality item such as a bottle, a tumbler or a pair of earphones is usually enough. Logo branding is available on many products, on request, and bags and bottles are the most common candidates. We recommend confirming your final attendee count, plus a small buffer for walk-ins, before placing the order so every delegate receives an identical kit.

Gifts for Speakers, Shareholders and Winners

Beyond delegates, most events have a smaller group of VIPs. Gifts for guest speakers should feel personal and premium: a Sennheiser or Bose headset, a Titan watch or a Swiss Military travel set. Keep quantities small and presentation polished.

AGM gifts for shareholders are often subject to cost guidelines, so practical items with a clear verified MRP, such as dinnerware, flasks or small appliances, are sensible. Annual day awards and sports day prizes for employees work well as tiered awards: a smartwatch or speaker for winners, earphones or bottles for runners-up, and a participation token for everyone else. For offsite gifts for employees and team days out, travel-friendly items like backpacks, power banks and tumblers are consistently popular.

Frequently asked questions

What are good corporate event gifts for large audiences?

Water bottles, tumblers, power banks, charging cables, earphones and backpacks are dependable because they are portable and used daily. For large audiences, choose one or two items and brand them where possible.

Can you deliver event gifts directly to the venue?

Yes. From Chandigarh, we deliver to event venues anywhere in India. In-stock items typically arrive in a few working days. Share venue details, a contact person and event date so we can plan delivery with a safe buffer.

Do you offer gifts for event attendees with our logo?

Logo branding is available on many products, on request, including bags, bottles and some tech accessories. We confirm branding method, minimum quantity and lead time for each item in the proposal.

What should we give winners at an annual day?

Tiered prizes work well: smartwatches, speakers or premium earbuds for winners, neckbands or power banks for runners-up, and bottles or tumblers for participants. We can prepare all tiers on one proposal.
