Bottles, Flasks and Lunch Boxes

Bottles and flasks are the most requested Cello products for corporate use. A cello bottle bulk order can range from simple steel bottles for large teams to vacuum-insulated flasks for managers and clients. When comparing, look at capacity, single or double wall construction, lid type and weight. Double-wall models keep drinks hot or cold for longer and feel more premium in hand.

Lunch boxes are the other everyday essential. A cello lunch box bulk order suits onboarding kits, factory and plant teams, and education sector programmes. Options include steel containers, insulated lunch boxes and multi-compartment sets with carry bags. Pairing a bottle with a lunch box makes a complete daily-use kit that works for almost any employee and stays within modest budgets.

Casseroles and Kitchenware for Festive Gifting

Casseroles are a favourite for Diwali, Durga Puja and wedding-season gifting because they are used at family meals and look good on the table. A cello casserole bulk order can include single insulated servers or sets of two or three sizes, which feel generous without stretching the budget. Glass and steel storage containers and serving sets extend the range further.

Cello kitchenware suits a wide audience: manufacturing teams value durable household products, and retail and FMCG companies use them for dealer and customer gifts. For a richer festive gift, pair a casserole set with a flask, or combine Cello storage with La Opala dinnerware. We quote such combinations within one proposal, with each item listed alongside its MRP, so approvals stay quick.

Frequently asked questions

Which Cello products are best for corporate gifting?

Insulated bottles, flasks, lunch boxes and casserole sets are the most popular. They are useful to employees and families alike, easy to ship and available across several price points. We recommend options based on your audience and per-person budget.

Can Cello bottles be branded with our logo?

Logo branding is available on many Cello bottles and flasks, on request, typically by printing or engraving on the body. We confirm feasibility for your chosen model and share any extra lead time in the proposal before you approve.

Are Cello products suitable for return gifts?

Yes. Bottles, tumbler sets, lunch boxes and casseroles are popular return gifts for weddings, housewarmings and company functions. They suit guests of all ages and are easy to pack. We suggest options at your budget per piece.

Can you supply Cello in mixed colours?

Many Cello products come in several colours, and we can split quantities across them subject to stock. Share your preferred split early, especially for large orders or branded runs, so we can confirm availability before dispatch.
