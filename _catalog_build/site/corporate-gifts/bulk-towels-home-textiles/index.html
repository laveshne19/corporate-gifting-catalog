Welspun Corporate Gifts: What the Range Covers

The Welspun range spans several formats. A towel set corporate gift might include a pair of bath towels, a bath-and-hand towel combination or a family set with face towels. A bedsheet gift set corporate option, usually a double bedsheet with pillow covers, feels more substantial and suits festive or client gifting. A dohar gift set makes a comfortable, lightweight gift for most seasons and regions, and pairs well with bedsheets.

When comparing, look at GSM for towels, which indicates weight and absorbency; thread count and fabric for bedsheets; and size, fill and fabric for dohars. Colour matters too, since neutral and pastel shades suit most homes. We list these details against MRP for each option, so you can match quality to budget. A welspun towel gift set works especially well in festive programmes because it is easy to pack, ship and distribute.

Where Home Textiles Work Best

A home textile gift set fits occasions where the recipient's family matters as much as the recipient. Diwali, Durga Puja and Pongal programmes often include towels or bedsheets, especially for manufacturing and plant teams. Housewarming gifts, wedding favours and retirement presents are other natural fits.

Textiles also combine well with other products. A towel set with a Cello flask, or a bedsheet set with La Opala dinnerware, creates a richer gift without a large jump in budget. For hospitality businesses, textiles align naturally with the industry, while for BFSI and IT firms they offer a welcome alternative to gadgets. Because textiles are light, they travel well and suit multi-location dispatch, which keeps freight simple for companies with many branches.

Frequently asked questions

Why choose towels or bedsheets as corporate gifts?

They are used daily, suit every household and are appreciated by families, not just the recipient. Textiles are also light and easy to ship, which helps with large and multi-location programmes, and the Welspun name adds assurance of quality.

What is GSM and why does it matter?

GSM stands for grams per square metre and indicates a towel's weight and density. Higher-GSM towels typically feel plusher and absorb more, while lower-GSM towels dry faster. We show GSM where available so you can compare sets.

Can textiles be combined with other gifts?

Yes. Towel sets pair well with flasks, bottles or dinnerware to create richer festive gifts. We can supply such combinations within one proposal and one GST invoice, with each component listed alongside its verified MRP.

Can we add our branding to towel sets?

Embroidery or printing on the textile itself depends on the product and quantity. Custom packaging or a branded card is available on many products, on request. We confirm the options for your chosen sets in the proposal.
