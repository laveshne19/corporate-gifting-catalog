What to Choose from the Portronics Range

Portronics products fall into a few categories that suit gifting well. Power banks are the most popular, particularly compact models for commuters and higher-capacity units for field staff and frequent travellers. Chargers and cables, including fast chargers and multi-port adapters, are useful add-ons for onboarding kits. Portable Bluetooth speakers cater to music lovers, while wireless keyboards, mice, stands and hubs suit desk-based teams.

When comparing power banks, look at capacity in mAh, output wattage, the number and type of ports, and weight. For speakers, compare output, battery life and water resistance. Because the range is broad, we shortlist a handful of models that suit your audience and budget rather than sending the entire catalogue, and we list the verified MRP beside each one.

Power Banks for Sales, Service and Field Teams

Power banks are one of the most practical corporate gifts in India, especially for people who spend the day on the move. A portronics power bank bulk order can be tiered by capacity: slim units for office staff and higher-capacity models for those on the road. They also pair well with a Portronics cable or charger to form a compact travel tech kit.

For onboarding and events, power banks take logo branding well on many models, on request, and custom packaging can be discussed. Airlines limit the capacity of power banks allowed in cabin baggage, so for employees who fly often it is worth choosing models that stay within typical limits. We state capacity clearly in every proposal so you can make that decision easily, and we flag which models suit frequent travellers.
