Choosing the Right Capacity and Specs

Capacity is the first decision. A 10000mAh power bank bulk order suits most recipients: it charges a smartphone roughly two times, is pocket-friendly and fits entry and mid-level budgets. A 20000mAh model is better for field sales teams, frequent travellers and anyone carrying multiple devices, though it is heavier.

Beyond capacity, compare output wattage and fast-charging support, the number and type of ports (USB-C input and output is now standard), build material, weight and safety protections. A magnetic power bank bulk order is worth considering for iPhone-heavy teams, since these snap on for wireless charging; availability varies by brand and model, so we confirm stock case by case. We can share a side-by-side comparison of shortlisted models to help your team decide.
