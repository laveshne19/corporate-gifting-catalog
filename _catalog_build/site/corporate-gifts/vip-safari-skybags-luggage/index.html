Comparing Luggage for Gifting

Luggage choices come down to size, shell and features. Cabin trolleys suit frequent business travellers, while medium and large check-in sizes suit families and holiday travel. Hard-shell polycarbonate or polypropylene trolleys resist knocks, while soft-sided luggage offers expandable capacity and external pockets.

Other specifications to compare include wheel type (four spinner wheels are easiest to manoeuvre), lock type (a TSA-approved lock matters for international travellers), handle quality and weight. For gifting, colour matters too; classic shades have broader appeal in large programmes. We can shortlist trolleys from VIP, Safari and Skybags within your budget and present specifications side by side, so you choose based on how your recipients will actually travel.

Brands and Where They Fit

Each brand has a slightly different personality. VIP is a long-established name that recipients across age groups recognise, which makes VIP corporate gifts a safe choice for long-service awards and senior staff. Safari is known for modern designs and good value across trolleys and backpacks; Safari corporate gifting often covers both luggage for managers and backpacks for wider teams.

Skybags corporate gifts lean younger, with bright designs and popular laptop backpacks that suit campus hires, onboarding kits and IT teams. A skybags bulk order for backpacks can form the base of a welcome kit, while a safari bags bulk order for trolleys can serve as a premium award within the same programme. Mixing brands by tier lets you keep choice and value balanced.
